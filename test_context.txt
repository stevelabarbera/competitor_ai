How Censys describes their Attack Surface Management (ASM):
Censys ASM discovers, inventories, and monitors your entire Internet exposure. The external attack
surface is assessed for risks and each is prioritized by what is important to you. An outside-in view, or
attacker’s perspective, of every asset and exposure is refreshed daily, hourly, or on-demand giving
your organization near-real time visibility and context so you can manage and communicate your
cybersecurity posture
